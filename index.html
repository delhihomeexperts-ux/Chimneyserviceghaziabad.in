Chimney & Hob Support for Everyday Cooking

Cooking produces grease, steam, odours and airborne particles. A well-maintained chimney can help extract cooking fumes, while a correctly functioning hob supports everyday meal preparation.

When a chimney becomes noisy, loses suction or stops working, the cause may involve grease buildup, a blocked filter, a duct restriction, an electrical fault or a motor issue. Inspection helps identify the next appropriate step.

Hob concerns can include ignition difficulties, uneven burner operation, control problems and cleaning needs. Gas-related faults require particular care and should be assessed by a suitably qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept Hafele chimney and hob service enquiries?

Yes, you can submit an enquiry for Hafele chimney and hob products in Ghaziabad. Availability for your specific model and locality must be confirmed.

How often should I clean my chimney?

It depends on cooking frequency, oil use, filter design and the manufacturer's instructions. Heavy cooking may require more frequent filter maintenance. Refer to the product manual for model-specific guidance.

Why is my chimney suction weak?

Possible causes include a dirty filter, restricted duct, unsuitable duct arrangement or a mechanical fault. An assessment is needed to determine the cause.

What causes unusual chimney noise?

Vibration, loose components, an obstruction or a motor-related problem can cause noise. Stop using the appliance if you suspect an electrical or mechanical safety issue.

Can you repair a hob with ignition problems?

You can submit an enquiry describing the symptoms. Gas appliances require appropriate expertise. If you smell gas, do not operate electrical switches or flames; turn off the gas supply only if safe, leave the area and contact your gas provider or emergency service.

How much does a repair cost?

Costs depend on the fault, model, parts and work involved. Ask for the inspection fee, estimated total and any additional charges before authorising the work.

Is a parts warranty available?

Warranty coverage is not automatic. Ask for the specific written terms, covered part, duration, exclusions and proof of purchase where applicable before proceeding.
