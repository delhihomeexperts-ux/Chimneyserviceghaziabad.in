Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide Hafele chimney repair in Ghaziabad?

We provide independent service enquiries for Hafele chimney appliances. Contact us to confirm the specific model and service availability.

Why is my kitchen chimney making noise?

Possible causes include vibration, loose fittings, grease buildup, an obstruction or a motor issue. The cause needs to be inspected.

When should a chimney be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on cooking habits, grease accumulation and the appliance manufacturer's maintenance instructions.

Can a clogged filter reduce suction?

Yes. Grease or other buildup can restrict airflow. A filter inspection and appropriate cleaning may help.

Do you offer a warranty on parts?
