Kitchen Chimney Service FAQs

Which chimney services can I enquire about?

You can ask about deep cleaning, filter care, routine maintenance, low suction, motor symptoms, unusual noise or vibration, lights and controls, and installation requirements. The exact service offered must be confirmed after reviewing your issue.

Do you cover Ghaziabad and Greater Noida?

We accept service enquiries for listed localities in Ghaziabad and Greater Noida. Actual coverage, travel availability and appointment timing must be confirmed for your address.

Why might my chimney have low suction?

Possible reasons include a dirty filter, airflow restriction, ducting conditions or a mechanical issue. These are possibilities, not a diagnosis; the appliance and installation should be assessed appropriately.

How often should a chimney be cleaned?

It depends on cooking frequency, oil and grease exposure, filter design and manufacturer guidance. Inspect filters regularly and follow the cleaning instructions for your specific model.

Can you guarantee original parts or manufacturer warranty?

No such guarantee is made on this website. Parts availability, source, compatibility, any part warranty and charges must be discussed and confirmed before work begins.
