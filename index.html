Hafele Chimney Service FAQs

Do you provide Hafele chimney service in Ghaziabad?

You can submit an enquiry with your chimney model and issue. We are an independent service provider, not an authorised Hafele service centre. Confirm service availability before booking.

When should a kitchen chimney be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on cooking habits, oil and grease exposure, filter type and manufacturer guidance. Reduced suction or visible grease may indicate that maintenance is needed.

Why is my chimney making unusual noise?

Possible causes include loose components, vibration, a blockage or a motor-related issue. Stop using the appliance if it seems unsafe and arrange a suitable inspection.

Can I request motor or suction troubleshooting?

Yes. Choose Motor / Suction Issue in the booking form and describe the symptoms. The actual cause and repair options can be discussed after assessment.
