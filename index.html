What if my chimney is making noise?

Unusual noise can have several causes, including loose components, vibration or motor-related issues. The cause should be checked before deciding on a repair.

Why is my chimney suction low?

A clogged filter, duct obstruction or other mechanical issue may reduce suction. The exact cause depends on the chimney and installation.
