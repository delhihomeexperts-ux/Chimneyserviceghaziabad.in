Kitchen Chimney Care respects the privacy of people who contact us about kitchen chimney cleaning, repair, installation and hob services. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be provided to us and how that information may be used.

1. Information You May Provide

When you call or message us, you may choose to share information needed to respond to your enquiry, such as:

  • Your name and contact number.
  • Your service address or locality.
  • Your appliance brand and model, if known.
  • Details of the chimney or hob problem.
  • Your preferred appointment date and time.

Please provide only the information reasonably necessary for your service enquiry. Do not send passwords, banking PINs or other confidential credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you choose to provide may be used to:

  • Respond to service enquiries and questions.
  • Discuss service availability and estimated charges.
  • Arrange or confirm appointments.
  • Communicate about repairs, parts and service updates.
  • Handle customer support and relevant disputes.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

3. Phone Calls, SMS and Booking Enquiries

Clicking a call or SMS link may open the calling or messaging application on your device. Your communications may be processed by your mobile network provider and the relevant application provider under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sending a message or making a call does not automatically confirm a service appointment. The appointment is confirmed only when the details have been agreed with you.

Unless a form explicitly states that it stores submissions, do not assume that information entered into a website form is saved as a booking record. Actual handling depends on the website's current technical configuration.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer contact information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to arrange a service, communicate with a requested service provider, operate the website or its technical services, comply with law, or protect legitimate rights and safety.

Any sharing should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Cookies and Technical Information

The website hosting provider may process technical information such as server logs to deliver, maintain and secure the website. External links, including map links, may be governed by the privacy practices of the relevant third-party service.

If analytics, advertising measurement, tracking pixels or other data-collection tools are added to this website, this policy must be updated to describe the tools actually used and any applicable consent choices.

6. Data Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect customer information against unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. However, no online communication or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain relevant business records, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. The actual retention period depends on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided or request correction or deletion where applicable law permits. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve legitimate business matters.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for people seeking household appliance services and is not directed at children. Please do not submit a child's personal information unless it is necessary and you are legally entitled to provide it.

10. External Websites

This website may contain links to third-party websites or applications. We do not control their privacy practices. Please review the privacy policy of any external service before sharing information with it.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, business practices or applicable legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
